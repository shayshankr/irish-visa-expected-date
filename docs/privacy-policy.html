Privacy Policy

App: Irish Visa Expected Date  ·  Last updated: 2 July 2026

Irish Visa Expected Date ("the app") is a tool for tracking Irish visa application timelines. This policy explains what data the app handles, in plain language.

1. Data stored only on your device

By default, everything you enter — embassy, visa application centre, submission date, visa type, passport expiry, biometric appointment date, saved applications, status history, notes, and tracked document expiry dates — is stored locally on your device using Android's DataStore, and is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes this data permanently.

The app also runs a background check (via Android WorkManager) that periodically loads the public Irish embassy decisions webpage for the embassy you selected, to detect updates. This only reads a public webpage — it does not transmit any of your personal data.

2. Optional: sharing an anonymous outcome to help other applicants

The "Community Times" feature lets you optionally share a decision outcome so other applicants can see aggregate wait-time statistics. Nothing is shared unless you explicitly tap "Share" and confirm.

If you choose to share, the following fields are sent to a Google Cloud Firestore database and are not linked to your name, email, or device:

No name, email address, passport number, notes, or device/advertising identifier is ever included in a shared record. Aggregate statistics (record counts and averages) are only shown once enough people have shared data for a given embassy/visa-type combination, so no individual submission can be singled out from small sample sizes.

Limitation on deletion: because shared records are anonymous and contain no identifier linking them back to you or your device, the app has no way to locate or delete a specific past submission on request. Only share an outcome if you're comfortable with that record remaining in the aggregate dataset indefinitely.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase Cloud Firestore — stores the optional anonymous submissions described above. Google's own privacy practices for Firebase/Google Cloud apply to this infrastructure: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Play In-App Review — the standard Play Store prompt asking you to rate the app; handled entirely by Google Play services, not by this app.
  • Chrome Custom Tabs — links such as "Book VAC appointment" or "Open Visa Decisions Page" open the relevant embassy/VAC website in an in-app browser tab. Once you follow such a link, that third-party site's own privacy policy applies.

The app does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or any tracking beyond what's described above.

4. Permissions

  • Internet — required for the optional community-sharing feature and for checking the embassy decisions page.
  • Notifications — used only for local reminders you enable in-app (e.g. decision window opening, overdue alerts). No notification content is sent to or from a server.

5. Children's privacy

This app is a utility for visa applicants and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
